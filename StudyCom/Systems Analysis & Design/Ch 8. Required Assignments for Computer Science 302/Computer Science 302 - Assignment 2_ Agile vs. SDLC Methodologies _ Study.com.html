About this Assignment

System Analysis and Design refers to the procedures of examining or developing business systems with the intent of improving it through industry standard methodology and framework. For this assignment, you will write one report that analyzes the Agile methodology and compares it to one other methodology.

Using course materials and external, peer-reviewed sources, write a 1500 to 2000 word paper. In this paper you will compare and contrast Agile versus 'standard' SDLC methodologies. Provide a thorough discussion on why Agile should be used over other methods (such as waterfall); outline drawbacks to Agile and where waterfall would be more appropriate.

Scenario

Backyard Adventures (BA) is a guided tour and water sports rental company in Jacksonville, Florida run by two brothers, Shawn and Harry Weaver. They advertise Backyard Adventures in several local and regional magazines and web sites, but the Weavers don't feel that it is attracting new business.

In the first assignment for this course, you have developed a systems analysis and design report for Backyard Adventures. You are now going to approach this systems implementation from a different perspective: Comparing Agile versus traditional SDLC methodologies (waterfall, spiral, etc.).

Writing the Report

Based upon the systems design you proposed in the previous paper, pick one 'traditional' SDLC methodology to compare/contrast to Agile. For example: Waterfall, Spiral, V-shaped.

In your paper, be sure to address the following:

  • Definition of Agile
  • Definition of your chosen traditional model
  • Pros and cons to each, providing examples and a thorough discussion
  • Recommendation for Backyard Adventures: Which methodology should they use in their new system?

When writing your recommendation, keep the following in mind when comparing/contrasting Agile to the other method:

  • Who are the end users of the system?
  • Are the requirements stable? Will they change a lot?
  • Where are the project teams located?
  • What is the size of the project?

These guiding questions can help you as you define a recommendation for Backyard Adventures.

Before You Submit

When you complete your assignment, we suggest taking some time to check for any errors or to add any finishing touches. We also suggest that you use online plagiarism checkers to make sure that your assignment is not too similar to any existing materials. Plagiarized submissions will NOT be graded.

How to Submit Your Assignment

When you are ready to submit your assignment, please fill out the submission form and attach your assignment as a Microsoft Word or text file. You should receive your assignment grade within 48 hours.

If you are not satisfied with the score you receive on your assignment, you may revise or rewrite it, and resubmit them for grading using the same submission form above. Keep in mind that the grade you receive on your assignment is only a portion of your overall grade for the course. Please see the course syllabus for a more detailed breakdown of the grading policy.
